GNU time reports the largest individual process RSS in the compilation tree, not the simultaneous sum of compiler processes. Its CPU time includes waited-for children. The psutil fallback samples simultaneous tree RSS and CPU counters, may count shared pages twice and may miss brief processes/peaks. These backends are not interchangeable memory measurements.

Wall time includes launching the measurement wrapper and compilation; excludes copying, hashing, compiler-version lookup and report writing. Repeated builds may benefit from OS cache; these are consecutive trials, not controlled cold-cache runs. No GPU metrics are measured.
