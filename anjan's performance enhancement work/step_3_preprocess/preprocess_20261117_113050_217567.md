GNU time RSS is the largest individual process peak, not simultaneous tree total. psutil RSS is sampled tree total (20 ms), can double-count shared pages and miss short peaks; its CPU counters may miss brief processes. A killed GNU time run may have no CPU/RAM result. Wall time includes launch/monitoring/termination overhead. No numerical equivalence is asserted. Output presence/hashes are provenance, not scientific validation.
