# Solar Physics - Research Topics

Each topic is an open-ended investigation: **learn the physics → explore literature and data → compute or simulate → find intermediate results → choose the next direction → visualize and interpret findings**. Reproducing known results is a valid starting point; original questions can emerge from there.

## 1. Why Do Some Solar Flares Erupt While Others Remain Confined?
Investigate how magnetic-field structure and evolution determine whether a flare launches a coronal mass ejection (CME). Compare observed events, calculate magnetic indicators, and test possible explanations.

## 2. Where Do Solar-Wind Magnetic Switchbacks Originate?
Study sudden reversals of the magnetic field measured by spacecraft. Analyze time-series data and investigate whether switchbacks arise near the Sun through reconnection or evolve through waves and turbulence.

## 3. Can Solar Flares Be Forecast Using Interpretable Physics?
Explore whether measurable magnetic properties can predict flare occurrence or strength. Build and evaluate transparent physical/statistical models, visualize failure cases, and refine hypotheses.

## 4. What Heats the Solar Corona to Millions of Degrees?
Examine candidate heating mechanisms, such as Alfvén-wave dissipation and nanoflares. Develop simplified physical models or simulations, compare predictions with observations, and identify their limitations.

## 5. What Can Surface Oscillations Reveal About the Solar Interior?
Use helioseismology to extract oscillation frequencies from solar data and explore how waves constrain interior structure. Attempt simplified inverse models and visualize inferred properties and uncertainties.

---

**Suggested starting topic:** **#1 — Confined vs. eruptive solar flares.** It combines accessible observations, magnetic physics, computation, visualization, and room for independent investigation.

**Potential data sources:** NASA Solar Dynamics Observatory (SDO), Parker Solar Probe, Solar Orbiter, and public solar-flare catalogs.
