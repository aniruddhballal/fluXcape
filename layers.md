• The Engine (Core): Spanning the inner 25%, this is a super-dense plasma soup of Hydrogen and Helium at 15 million °C. It is the only place hot enough to overcome electrical repulsion and fuse Hydrogen into Helium ash, actively creating all the Sun's photons.
• The Pinball Wall (Radiative Zone): Spanning from 25% to 70% of the radius, this layer is packed so tightly by gravity that the plasma cannot physically move. It is completely opaque, forcing photons to crash, scatter, and "random-walk" for 1 lakh years just to escape it. The entire layer spins smoothly as a single, rigid solid body.
• The Interface (Tachocline): A razor-thin friction boundary between the rigid radiative zone and the loose convective zone above. Because the layers rub past each other at different speeds, this layer acts like a massive mechanical blender that shears and amplifies the global magnetic fields.
• The Blender (Convective Zone): Spanning from 70% to 100% of the radius, the pressure here finally drops enough for the plasma to become "loose" and physically boil like a pot of water. Because it is a churning soup of moving electric charges, it operates the solar dynamo, creating the magnetic fields. As plasma rises, its density plummets drastically until it is thousands of times lighter than Earth's air.
• The State Transition (Photosphere): The visible 500 km "surface skin." Here, the plasma cools to 5,500°C, causing a major atomic change: subatomic free electrons snap back onto nuclei to form neutral, normal gas atoms. Because neutral gas is transparent, photons finally break free and fly into space as sunshine.
• The Shock Absorber (Chromosphere): A thin 2,000 km atmospheric transition zone. Because it sits between a dense surface and a near-vacuum corona, it acts as a density shock absorber where sound and magnetic waves from the boiling surface squeeze together and steepen into violent shockwaves.
• The Magnetic Halo (Corona): The vast outer atmosphere. It is a near-vacuum dominated by magnetic fields. When the randomly braided and twisted magnetic loops rooted in the convective zone get squeezed too hard, they snap via magnetic reconnection, releasing massive CMEs and heating this halo to millions of degrees.
